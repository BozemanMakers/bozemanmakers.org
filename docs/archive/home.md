# Welcome

[10 Innovation Ln Unit D Bozeman, MT 59718](https://goo.gl/maps/jH1bcA8uk3vs2Th87) (Rear entrance of building)

The Bozeman Makerspace is a [Makerspace ](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makerspace)located in Bozeman, MT. If you have an
interest in making or modding things, or just want to learn how things work, this is the place for you.

Our Makerspace is a workshop combined with a meeting space where people of similar interests or skills can converge to
create, modify, learn, talk and build.

A Makerspace allows groups of people to pool resources and create a community of people with varied interests. These
interests may include but are definitely not limited to: circuitry, robotics, soldering, woodworking, fabricating,
programming, networking, hacking, bending, etc.

* * *

## Membership

**Interested in joining the Makerspace?** Come tour the Makerspace during a Thursday evening Open Build event (check our
calendar). We can answer any questions you might have at that time.

Members will need a signed [Release Form](https://bozemanmakers.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/bms-member-and-guest-
release-form.pdf "bms-member-and-guest-release-form"), and must be 18 or older. Kids are welcome with their parents.

We handle new member walkthroughs and key fob sign-out at Thursday evening Open Build events. If you sign up for a
membership, please plan on attending the next Open Build.

## Donate

Would you like to make a tax deductible donation to the Bozeman Makerspace? Please do so using the PayPal button below.
Any amount is helpful to keeping the space open and operating. Check or cash donations are also accepted at Makerspace
events. [Contact an officer](https://bozemanmakers.wordpress.com/contact/) for details.
