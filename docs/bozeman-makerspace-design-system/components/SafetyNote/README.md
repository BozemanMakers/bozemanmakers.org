# SafetyNote
A callout with a hazard stripe across its top, for shop rules, PPE reminders and anything people must not skim past.

- Default: brass/ink hazard stripe. `bzm-note--info` swaps in a solid `gauge` band for neutral info ("Parking is around back").
- Lead with a bold, plain sentence in the display face, then one or two lines of detail. No exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS.
- Don't use it for marketing. If everything is a safety note, nothing is.

The consumer provides: the heading sentence and body text.
